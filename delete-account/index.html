Account Deletion Request

We respect your right to privacy. If you would like to delete your GeoTrace account and all associated personal data, please follow the steps below.

📧 How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to our support team with the following details:

📩 Email us at:

geomeasuregpsareacalculator@gmail.com

Please include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request – GeoTrace
  • The email address linked to your GeoTrace account
  • Your Google account name used to sign in (if applicable)

⏱ Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving your email. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

🗑 What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Deleted — Firebase authentication account
  • Deleted — All saved measurements, projects, and area records
  • Deleted — Your Firestore profile and personal user data
  • Deleted — Push notification tokens (Firebase Messaging)

📦 What Data Is Retained

  • Retained — Purchase records are retained by Google Play in accordance with their own privacy policy
  • Retained — Anonymized, non-identifiable analytics data that cannot be linked back to you

🔒 Data Retention Period

All personal data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted within 30 days of your verified deletion request. Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot identify you.

💬 Questions? If you have any concerns about your data or this process, contact us at: geomeasuregpsareacalculator@gmail.com
