This page explains how users of GPS Land & Acreage Calculator (package com.geotrace.app) developed by Vireo Innovations can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Email us at:

geomeasuregpsareacalculator@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request – GPS Land & Acreage Calculator
  • Email address used in the app
  • Your Google account name (if you signed in with Google)

Processing Time

We will verify your request and permanently delete your account within 30 days. A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion has been completed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile information stored in Cloud Firestore
  • Saved measurements
  • Saved projects
  • Land area records
  • Cloud backups associated with your account
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notification) tokens

Data That May Be Retained

  • Google Play purchase records maintained by Google
  • Anonymous analytics data that cannot identify you
  • Information that must be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations, if any

Privacy

Once your deletion request has been processed, your personal account data cannot be recovered.
