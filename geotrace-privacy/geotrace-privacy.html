01

Overview

GeoTrace ("we", "us", or "our") is a GPS-based land measurement application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

✅Short version: GeoTrace does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data stays on your device. We do not require account creation or login.

By using GeoTrace, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

03

Location Data

GeoTrace uses your device's GPS to provide land measurement functionality. Location access is the core feature of the app.

  • Location is accessed only while you are actively using the app
  • GPS coordinates are used solely to place measurement points on the map
  • Your location data is never transmitted to any server
  • Your location data is never shared with third parties
  • Saved measurement coordinates are stored only on your device
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings
📡GeoTrace works fully offline. When measuring in offline mode, GPS coordinates are used locally and no internet connection is required or used.

05

Data Storage & Security

All data generated within GeoTrace — measurements, project names, GPS points, and exported files — is stored exclusively on your device using Android's local storage system.

  • No data is uploaded to any cloud server
  • No remote database or backend server exists
  • Deleting the app removes all associated data from your device
  • We have no ability to access your data remotely
  • We recommend enabling device-level encryption for best security
06

Third-Party Services

GeoTrace uses the following third-party services only for map tile display:

  • Google Maps / OpenStreetMap — Map tile imagery (when online). These services may collect standard request logs per their own privacy policies.
  • Google Play Services — Required for Android device GPS functionality.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, crash reporting services, or social media integrations. GeoTrace contains no advertisements.

07

Children's Privacy

GeoTrace does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal information from any user, the app is safe for general use. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.
