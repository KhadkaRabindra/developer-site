Delete Your Account

Application: Third Wheel

Package Name: com.impactit.thirdwheel

Developer: Impact IT

This page explains how users of Third Wheel (package com.impactit.thirdwheel) developed by Impact IT can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Email us at:

support@thirdwheel.com.np

Please include:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request – Third Wheel
  • Email address or phone number used in the app

Processing Time

We will verify your request and permanently delete your account within 30 days. A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion has been completed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account and login information
  • Your profile information
  • Photos and videos you uploaded
  • Messages and content associated with your account
  • Push notification tokens associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

  • Google Play purchase records maintained by Google
  • Anonymous analytics data that cannot identify you
  • Information that must be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations, if any

Privacy

Once your deletion request has been processed, your personal account data cannot be recovered.

Contact

For questions regarding your account or privacy:

support@thirdwheel.com.np
